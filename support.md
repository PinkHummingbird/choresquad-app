# Chore Squad: Support

[Chore Squad home](./) · [Privacy Policy](privacy)

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: **[choresquad.app.support@gmail.com](mailto:choresquad.app.support@gmail.com)**

We reply within two working days. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you tapped.

## Common questions

**How do I add my family?** Open Squad, add each child's profile, then tap Invite a member › Create invite link › Send invite. Everyone, including kids from age 4, joins on their own iPhone by tapping the link and then their name. A child under 13 needs their iPhone signed in to a Child Account from Family Sharing. A child without an iPhone can use a profile on yours: turn on "Let a child use this device" in Settings. From version 1.0.1, if you send the link before adding a child, they can tap "I'm a kid and I'm not on the list" and enter their name and age; you can edit or remove the profile in Squad.

**The invite doesn't work.** Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in the iPhone's Settings, with iCloud Drive on, then try again.

**Not getting notifications?** (version 1.0.1 and later) Open Squad › Settings and tap Turn on notifications. If you said no before, that opens the iPhone's Settings: turn on Allow Notifications for Chore Squad. Quiet hours and a daily limit per child are under Squad › Settings too.

**Does the app move money?** No. Balances are a record of what's been earned. You settle up in real life, outside the app.

**What does Free include?** 1 parent, 1 child, 3 new chores and 3 finished chores a week, 1 Squad Goal and 7 days of Insights. In version 1.0.1 and later, a child can always tap "I did it!": the weekly limit applies when a parent approves, and any extra chores wait in Requests until next week, or until you upgrade in Squad › Your plan.

**How do I restore Pro?** Squad › Your plan › See Pro › Restore. Pro is shared through Family Sharing.

**How do I use an offer code?** In the app (version 1.0.1 and later): Squad › Your plan › See Pro › Redeem a code. Or in the App Store app: tap your picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

**How do I cancel Pro?** In the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**How do I delete our data?** Squad › Delete my profile, or Delete household & all data (owner only). See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
