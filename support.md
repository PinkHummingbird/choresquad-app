# Chore Squad: Support

[Chore Squad home](./) · [Privacy Policy](privacy)

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: **[choresquad.app.support@gmail.com](mailto:choresquad.app.support@gmail.com)**

We reply within two working days. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you tapped.

## Common questions

**How do I add my family?** Open Squad and tap Invite. Each person joins on their own device with their own Apple Account. A younger child can use a profile on your phone instead: turn on "Let a child use this device" in Settings.

**The invite doesn't work.** Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in the iPhone's Settings, with iCloud Drive on, then try again.

**Does the app move money?** No. Balances are a record of what's been earned. You settle up in real life, outside the app.

**How do I restore Pro?** Squad › Your plan › See Pro › Restore. Pro is shared through Family Sharing.

**How do I cancel Pro?** In the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

**How do I delete our data?** Squad › Delete my profile, or Delete household & all data (owner only). See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
