# Chore Squad: Privacy Policy

[Chore Squad home](./) · [Support](support)

_Last updated: 17 November 2026 (notifications; a child invited by a parent's link can add their own profile)_

**Contact:** [choresquad.app.support@gmail.com](mailto:choresquad.app.support@gmail.com)

Chore Squad is a family chore app. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how to delete it. In short: **your family's data stays in your family's own iCloud. We never receive it and cannot see it.**

## Summary

- **No account of ours.** There is no sign-up; families share a household through iCloud.
- **No servers of ours.** We hold no copy of anything you enter.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.**

## What the app stores

- Each member's display name, character (avatar) and role (parent or child), and a child's age.
- Chores, rewards, balances, streaks, badges, goals and their history.
- Photos a member takes or picks as proof that a chore is done.
- Settings for your household and this device.

The app does not ask for or store email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or payment details.

## Where it's stored

Everything is stored on your devices and in your family's own iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit. Members of your household see the household's shared data through iCloud sharing. Apple processes this data under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We can't see or access it.

A parent passcode, if you set one, is kept only in this device's Keychain and is not included in iCloud backups.

## Notifications

From version 1.0.1, Chore Squad can send notifications, for example when a chore is due or ready to check, if you allow them. Your iPhone writes each notification itself. When something changes in your household, iCloud only signals your devices to check; that signal carries none of your family's data, and nothing passes through us. You can turn notifications off in the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Chore Squad.

## Children

Chore Squad is made for families with children aged 4 and up. A parent sets up the household and usually creates each child's profile. From version 1.0.1, a child who opens a parent's invitation link can add their own name and age to that household; the parent sees the profile in Squad and can edit or remove it. Children can't create a household, sign up, or invite anyone. A child can join from their own iPhone through the parent's invitation, signed in to their own Apple Account; for a child under 13 that is a Child Account the parent creates in Apple's Family Sharing. Chore Squad never creates an account or asks a child for an email address, phone number or password. A child without their own iPhone uses a profile on a parent's device instead. We collect only what chores need, and none of it reaches us.

## Purchases

Chore Squad Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the app only receives confirmation of what was bought.

## How long data is kept

- Proof photos are deleted when the reward is released, or 7 days after approval, depending on your household's setting.
- Chore details older than 60 days are cleared; totals and balances are kept.
- Everything else stays until you delete it.

## Deleting your data

In the app, open Squad. Any member can choose **Delete my profile**. The household's owner can choose **Delete household & all data**, which removes it from iCloud for everyone. A parent can also remove another member. You can export a copy of your data first. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and say what changed.
