# Chore Squad: Family Cleaning

**Make chores a family team effort.** Parents add chores and rewards, kids mark them done and save up, and everyone works toward shared Squad Goals.

Chore Squad is a family chore app for iPhone. Each family member joins on their own device, or a younger child uses a profile on a parent's phone. Everything syncs through your family's own iCloud. No ads, no tracking, and no server of ours.

**[Get Chore Squad on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6814967536)**. It's free to download for iPhone; Chore Squad Pro is an optional upgrade.
